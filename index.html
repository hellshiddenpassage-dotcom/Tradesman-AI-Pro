Tradesman AI

Tradesman AI is a software platform built for contractors, mechanics, equipment operators, and skilled trades professionals.

The platform provides AI-powered tools designed to help trades businesses operate more efficiently, produce professional documentation, and manage job workflows in one place.

Platform Features

  • Estimate generator for construction and service work
  • Scope of work writer for job proposals
  • Invoice and quote builder
  • Contract builder for service agreements
  • Equipment and mechanical troubleshooting assistant
  • Company pricing configuration for accurate estimates
  • Saved customers and job records
  • Professional PDF export for all generated documents
